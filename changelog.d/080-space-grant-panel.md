- **F080 (D-054):** a table owner can now let a room read one projection of their table. There was no `space.grant` command at all: grants are written on the source table with `source.grantProjection`, and the app could only read them. The new panel sends that command with both the room's workspace id and its slug (the authority compares the workspace, so a slug-only payload would grant nothing), and never widens a grant by itself: the port, resource, fields and row cap are all chosen by the person. Four unit tests cover the payload, including the refusal of a slug-only grant.
